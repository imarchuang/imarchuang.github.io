Explore · 阅读要点（原创改写，不是课文逐字）

How two friends celebrate

A FRIEND IN CHINA
The Spring Festival is coming. We will clean the house. I will help make jiaozi. Then I will watch a lion dance. We will visit family and have a big meal. This is hard work, but it will bring good luck.
A FRIEND IN THE UK
That sounds fun! Our main festival is Christmas. We put up a tree. On Christmas Eve I put a stocking by my bed. In the morning there are gifts. My grandparents come. First we open gifts. Then we eat a big meal.

Talk: How do the children and their families celebrate festivals?

Grammar in use

Talk about the future with will

I will / I’ll

I will help my grandma. I’ll watch a lion dance.

We will / We’ll

We’ll clean our house. We will have a big meal.

It will

It will be the Spring Festival next month. It will bring good luck.

In one hour, I will be at my grandma’s.

will = 将要。后面接动词原形：will eat / will visit / will watch。

Think and write · p.81

Plan a festival day

哪一个节日快到了？用 will 写一天的计划。可先抄范文，再改成自己家的。

Model:

It will be the Mid-Autumn Festival in September. I will eat mooncakes with my family. We will look at the beautiful moon. We will visit my grandparents and have a big meal together.

Think more about the Big Question

How do we celebrate festivals?

Special activities

We clean the house, watch lion dances, look at lanterns, or race dragon boats.

Traditional food

We make and enjoy jiaozi, mooncakes, zongzi, and yuanxiao.

Family

We visit relatives and have a big meal together.
