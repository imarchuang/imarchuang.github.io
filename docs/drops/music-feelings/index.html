词汇与情绪 · 知识点

Eight feeling words

happy 开心

A bright pop song can make us smile.

sad 难过

A slow piano piece may make us quiet.

angry 生气

Loud drums can sound like a storm.

afraid 害怕

Dark, low sounds can make us jump.

excited 兴奋

Fast beats make us want to move.

worried 担心

Soft, uneasy music can sit in our chest.

nervous 紧张

Before a show, even a familiar song may shake us.

surprised 惊讶

A sudden high note can make us look up.

同一首歌，不同的人可能有不同的感觉。先学会这些词，再去听、去说。

词汇与情绪 · 练习

Choose the best feeling

先自己想，再点模糊答案核对。

1. After a long day, Mei puts on a slow song. She closes her eyes and smiles.
She feels relaxed / happy.
2. The lights go out. A low sound starts. The class sits very still.
Some students feel afraid / nervous.
3. The school band plays a fast song. Everyone claps and stands up.
They feel excited.
4. Leo hears a sudden whistle in the middle of a quiet piece.
He feels surprised.
词汇与情绪 · 拔高题

Same song, different hearts

Read and discuss. There is no single “right” feeling.

A rainy-day violin piece makes Nora think of her grandma’s kitchen. It makes Kai think of a sad film. Both students are listening to the same track.

Talk: Why can one piece of music make two people feel different things?

Possible ideas: memories, time of day, how we feel before we listen, the stories in our heads.

拔高：音乐不只“规定”情绪，也叫醒我们自己的画面。

音乐类型与听力 · 知识点

Six kinds of music

classical

Orchestra, piano, violin. Often calm or grand.

country

Stories about home, roads, and everyday life.

folk

Old songs people share. Simple and warm.

jazz

Swing, surprise notes, room to improvise.

pop

Catchy choruses you can sing on the bus.

rock

Strong guitar and drums. Full of energy.

We don’t match one type to one feeling forever — we notice a usual mood.

Suggested classroom key (not the only truth):

音乐类型与听力 · 练习 + 拔高

Complete the talk

A: What’s your favourite kind of music?
B: I like folk music. It’s soft. It makes me feel calm / peaceful.
A: How about rock?
B: Rock is loud / fast / exciting. It makes me want to run.
Stretch 拔高

Give a reason with because: I like jazz because the notes keep changing. It makes me feel curious.

对话与角色扮演 · 知识点

Lunchtime Music Club

原创短对话（不是课本听力原文）。两人讨论社团和周五小演出。

NOAH
Are you coming to the music club after lunch? We’re choosing songs for Friday’s school show.
RINA
I want to, but I’m a bit nervous. I haven’t played in front of the class before.
NOAH
We can start with a slow folk song. It makes people feel calm. Then we can add a pop chorus.
RINA
That sounds good. Fast pop makes me excited — maybe that’s better for the last song.
对话 · 练习

Listen in your head, then answer

1. Where will they meet? At the music club after lunch
2. Why is Rina nervous? She hasn’t played in front of the class before
3. What kind of song do they choose first? A slow folk song
4. What will the last song probably be like? Fast pop — exciting
对话与角色扮演 · 拔高

Role-play cards

Student A

Invite your partner to a Saturday street concert. Ask what they want to hear and why.

Student B

You feel tired. You prefer slow music. Explain how it makes you feel and what you imagine.

Must use

I’d like to… / It makes me… / I’ll imagine… / I’m a little…

Switch roles after two minutes. Then change the setting: before a test / on a holiday bus.

阅读 · 知识点

A class post: My three tracks

FROM TARA · CLASS FORUM · ORIGINAL TEXT

Track 1. In the morning I put on a bright pop song. The beat is fast. It makes me feel awake and ready for P.E.

Track 2. When I do homework, I choose soft classical piano. It doesn’t shout. It helps me stay with the page.

Track 3. Before I sleep, I listen to a folk lullaby my uncle recorded. It is slow. I imagine a river under the moon. The same notes make my little brother laugh — he says they sound like frogs!

Music doesn’t give us one feeling only. It opens a door. What we see behind the door is ours.

阅读 · 练习

Match the paragraph idea

Track 1 is mainly about music and energy / morning feelings.
Track 2 is mainly about music and focus / homework.
Track 3 is mainly about music and imagination / different listeners.
The last line means: the same music can paint different pictures for different people.

语法 -ed / -ing · 知识点

How we feel vs how music is

-ed → people

I am excited. She looks interested. We feel relaxed. He was moved. They were amazed.

形容词修饰人的感受。

-ing → the music / the thing

The song is exciting. Jazz can be interesting. Slow piano is relaxing. The film music is moving. The ending is amazing.

形容词修饰事物的特点。

The concert was exciting, so the students were excited.

语法 · 拔高题

Fix the mix-up

Each sentence has one wrong adjective. Say the correct one.

“The sleepy cat looks relaxing on the sofa.” → relaxed (the cat is a listener / a being)
“I am exciting about the talent show.” → excited
Write one pair of your own: The music is ____. I am ____. e.g. interesting / interested
