Explore · 阅读要点（课堂转述，不是课文原文）

Natural resources

What they are

Air, water, wood and land. The Earth gives them to us.

自然资源

Why we need them

Living things need clean air and water. Plants need water and land. We use wood for paper, chairs and houses.

How we hurt them

Smoke from cars and factories makes air dirty. Rubbish makes land and water dirty. Dirty air can make people sick.

We should use things longer, reduce what we use, and recycle rubbish.

Grammar in use · p.67

should / shouldn’t

We should + 动词原形

We should recycle things.

We should turn off lights.

should not = shouldn’t

We should not hurt our natural resources.

We shouldn’t cut down trees.

建议别人：We should … / We shouldn’t …

后面不要加 to，也不要加 -s。We should recycle. 不是 We should to recycle.

How can we help the river?

Ducks, fish, birds and other animals live in the river.

听录音核对。

听录音核对。若录音不是 plant trees，以录音为准。

Say: Liu Xin’s family help clean the river near their home. They pick rubbish out of the river. They plant trees near the river.

Think and write · p.69

Let’s take care of the Earth

The Earth is important for all of us. It gives us natural resources. It is sick now. Let’s help it get better.

We should turn off the lights.

We should recycle bottles and put rubbish in the bin.

We shouldn’t cut down trees or throw rubbish into the river.

Let’s all make the Earth a better place.

Big Question

Why should we take care of the Earth?

Gifts

The Earth gives us natural resources.

Limits

Those resources are limited.

Actions

There are different ways to help.

Recycle

Recycling turns rubbish into new things.

Think: How does recycling help? We use fewer new resources, and less rubbish stays on the land and in the water.
